Good to know.

What do I need to get started?

You’ll need an Android TV, Google TV or Android-based Fire OS device. Use an AI agent that can run local commands on your computer. The agent helps you connect, and you approve the initial debugging request on your TV.

How do TV updates work?

OpenNava checks signed releases from the GitHub project. Your paired Android companion or setup agent can install a TV update. Your device may ask you to confirm the installation.

Do I need the phone app?

Both interfaces work with your TV remote. Install the optional Android companion and pair it with your TV whenever you like.
